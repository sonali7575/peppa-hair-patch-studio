How much does a hair patch cost?

Pricing depends on the selected system, customisation and service requirements. Contact Peppa for current options and a quote.

Can I book a consultation before deciding?

Yes. Send an enquiry on WhatsApp to ask about consultation availability. Your appointment is confirmed only after the studio responds.

Do you offer options for both men and women?

We welcome enquiries from both men and women. Contact the studio to confirm which options are currently available for your needs.

How do I care for a hair system?

Care routines depend on the system and attachment method. Ask the studio for the recommended maintenance and aftercare instructions for your chosen option.

How long does a hair patch last?

Lifespan varies with the product, use, maintenance and care. The studio can explain expected upkeep for the options it offers.
